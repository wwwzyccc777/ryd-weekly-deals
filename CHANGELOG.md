# Changelog

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-10

- Show all active offers by default and when selecting a store; make pagination counts explicit.
- Include Lidl kitchen, fashion and home campaigns with start-date labels.
- Parse City Gross public leaflet PDFs into product cards, with hidden-layout filtering, page references, cent prices, bundles, PRIO conditions and price-validity dates.
- Keep percent discounts distinct from sale prices, convert per-hectogram prices to per-kilogram prices and preserve original labels.
- Review new Chinese and English grocery translations and retain full official leaflet links.


## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-10

- Added ICA Maxi Linköping and nationwide same-price Lidl offers for the verified Linköping store.
- Added City Gross Tornby official leaflets; product prices remain unparsed and excluded from recommendations.
- Added bilingual email feedback, retailer membership labels, official images and truthful date / pricing displays.
- Extended anonymous cloud sync and failed-source retention to all six sources.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-09

- Public Chinese / English website for three Linköping stores.
- One-click official-source cloud sync with persistent R2 storage, concurrency lock and cooldown.
- Reviewed translations, original Swedish text and official image fallbacks.
- Explainable recommendations, store grouping, shopping lists and CSV export.
- MIT source distribution, deployment guide and automated build / tests.
